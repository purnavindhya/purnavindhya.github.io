[cite_start]I am a PhD student in the Department of Civil & Systems Engineering at Johns Hopkins University [cite: 7, 10][cite_start], advised by Prof. Lori Graham-Brady[cite: 38]. [cite_start]My research lies at the intersection of deep learning and computational solid mechanics[cite: 32]. [cite_start]I focus on developing generative AI models, such as denoising diffusion probabilistic models and neural operators, to predict full-field material stress responses and quantify uncertainties in multi-scale material design[cite: 32, 40]. 

[cite_start]Before starting my PhD, I worked as a Research Engineer at LTIMindtree in Bangalore, India, where I developed quantum annealing-based optimization tools and interpretable time series forecasting models[cite: 43, 45, 46]. [cite_start]I hold a Master of Technology in Civil Engineering from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru [cite: 11, 12][cite_start], and a Bachelor of Technology in Civil Engineering from the National Institute of Technology (NIT), Andhra Pradesh[cite: 13, 14].

### Research Interests
* [cite_start]**Machine Learning for Mechanics:** Generative AI (Diffusion Models, VAE, GAN), Neural Operators (PINN, DeepONet), and Graph Neural Networks (GNN)[cite: 32].
* [cite_start]**Uncertainty Quantification:** Gaussian Processes, Bayesian Neural Networks, and Active Learning for material discovery[cite: 32, 42].
* [cite_start]**Computational Solid Mechanics:** Multi-scale modeling, additive manufacturing materials, and finite element analysis[cite: 29, 37, 40].
